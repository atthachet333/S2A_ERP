==================================================

2. User clicks:
   "+ เพิ่มหน่วยใหม่"

"มีหน่วยรหัส ml อยู่แล้ว"

   - row inputs too small
   - dropdown too narrow
   - search input tiny
   - modal too small
   - labels hard to scan

==================================================
